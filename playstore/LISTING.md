## Full description (4000 char limit)

```
echoGalaxy is a free tool for exploring the universe — a ladder of five
scales you can climb with your fingers, from the surface of a planet to
the galaxies of the Local Group. Everything you see is generated live
on your device: no photos, no videos — real astronomy, computed in
front of you.

THE SCALE JOURNEY
• Planet — eight worlds: a rocky planet with city lights and a tidally
  locked moon, a molten lava world, a cracked ice world, a banded gas
  giant, a living star, a black hole with a Doppler-beamed accretion
  disc, and Saturn twice — once wearing its rings, and once the rings
  alone, a sheet of orbiting snowballs proportionally thinner than
  paper.
• Star System — four worlds on true Kepler orbits around their star.
  And here you become the physics: GRAB A PLANET AND THROW IT. Too slow
  and it falls into the star. Too fast and it escapes forever. In
  between, it finds a new orbit — Newton's cannonball, made playable,
  with a live dial that names your throw's fate before you let go.
• Nebula — the Pillars of Creation, light-years of dust where stars are
  hatching right now, eroded by the very light that reveals them.
• Galaxy — spiral, barred, elliptical, and irregular galaxies, every
  star's color computed from real blackbody physics.
• Local Group — our actual neighbourhood: the Milky Way, Andromeda,
  Triangulum, and their companions.

REAL SCIENCE, TOLD SIMPLY
Every object comes with honest facts sourced from real astronomy: why
the Moon always shows one face, why Saturn's rings are younger than
some dinosaurs and already disappearing, what the Roche limit builds
and destroys, and how a moon of Jupiter proved that not everything
orbits the Earth. Written for curious kids and their adults.

MADE TO BE TOUCHED
One finger orbits. A pinch zooms. Pinch past the edge of any scale and
you climb to the next one. Works offline after the first visit. No
ads, no accounts, no data collection — just the universe.
```
